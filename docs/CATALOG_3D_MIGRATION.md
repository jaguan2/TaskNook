# Catalog 3D asset migration

Generated by the furniture authoring pipeline. This tracks standalone GLB asset
coverage. The game now consumes the 179 furniture/prop GLBs; customized
people and pets retain their SVG rigs. See [live integration](ROOM_MODEL_INTEGRATION.md). Sections follow
the actual catalog order. A section is reviewed as a family before proceeding.

Sections with complete export coverage: **Seating & beds, Tables & desks, Storage, Rugs & floor, Light & warmth, Plants & greenery, Decoration, Tech & music, Architecture, On the wall, Kitchen, Food & drink, Autumn, Winter, Spring, Summer, Outdoors, Living things**.
