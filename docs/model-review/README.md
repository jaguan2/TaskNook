# Model review captures

Comparison sheets use the real SVG artwork with the scene's paint definitions
and default theme colours. They are captured against a light neutral background
so plant structure, glass, cabinet recesses and clothing overlaps are visible.

- [Plants](plants.webp), [storage](storage.webp), [lighting](lighting.webp):
  complete entries in those catalog groups, including pieces not used in presets.
- [Seated front](seated-front.webp) and [seated rear](seated-back.webp): all eight
  authored looks on the restored seated rig, each in break, focus and idle poses,
  at a chair height of 19.
- [Live Loft focus](loft-focus.webp): the built SPA with its actual focus timer
  running, showing the established seated rig after reverting the rejected
  perspective prototype. A coherent new seated silhouette remains future work.
- [Seating comparison](seating-comparison.webp): the blocky armchair/sofa,
  desk chair and garden bench before and after the seating follow-up.
- [Seating colours](seating-colours.webp): cream, dark and sage, front/rear.
- [Seating contact front](seating-contact-front.webp) and [rear](seating-contact-back.webp):
  body extremes on those four pieces in idle, focus and break, using the
  room's seat anchors and original character rig.

These are dated review snapshots, not finished reference-quality models.
The October 4 seating/contact captures retain their earlier resident proportions;
screenshots 37/38 and [the October handoff](../archive/HANDOFF-2026-10-06.md) show the compact
character baseline. Regenerate contact views before the next seating-art pass.
See [the findings](../MODEL-REVIEW.md) and [per-item backlog](../MODELING_ROADMAP.md).
Generate the complete catalog and wardrobe locally with `cd frontend` followed
by `npm run art`, then open `frontend/art-sheet/index.html`. That sheet has
fixtures across all catalog families and the character registries.
